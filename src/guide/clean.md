# Clean: converge

The chosen direction, clear enough to agree on before anyone spends time on polish. Read `--skill design` and apply its structure and sizing. Draw with `--style clean`: the server lays down the medium (straight lines, Helvetica); colour is yours.

- **The chosen direction only**, one idea per frame.
- **A wireframe with a point of view:** greys for structure, a primary colour for what's in focus, a secondary for a second kind of thing, red for danger and green for success where they mean that. No decoration; an image area is a grey box captioned with what goes there.
- A grid: aligned edges, even spacing (`layout`), hierarchy by size and weight.
- Real labels and representative data, not "Label" or lorem.
- Decisions and open questions as notes beside it.
- Done when people can agree on what's where and what happens next, and `obeya lint` is clean.
