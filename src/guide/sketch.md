# Sketch: rough and fast

Marker and coloured pencil: the look of an idea that's cheap to throw away. Read `--skill design` for framing and structure; skip the craft. Draw with `--style sketch`: the server lays down the hand-drawn medium (rough strokes, Excalifont).

- **The feel of a marker sheet:** bold strokes, boxes, words, arrows, sticky notes, quick doodles. Short labels; no polish, no nudging pixels.
- **Mostly black and white; colour when it means something** or to draw the eye (the one you lean to, a risk, what changed), not to decorate. Light tints, not heavy solid fills.
- **Library pieces are fine** (icons, devices, UI kits) when they read as sketched: insert with `--style sketch`.
- Draw the look of the thing; detail and states come later, if at all.
- Notes and assumptions as sticky notes; leave room for the person to draw.
- Done when it reads; neatness doesn't count.
